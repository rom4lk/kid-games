Games for kids

Small browser games. They need no install, no build and no internet — just open a card below.

  • 📖

    Living Words

    Read a word aloud and pick the matching picture. Levels by word length, story chapters of ten words.

    READING
  • 🧭

    Island of Discovery

    Walk the island one step a day, gather apples, logs and ideas, build a garden, a workshop and a library, and finish with a festival.

    STRATEGY
  • 🔥

    Forest Light

    Gather two branches and a stone, follow the glowing path back, and build a campfire. No reading and no way to lose.

    NO READING
  • 🧱

    Cube Island

    Take plank blocks from the tray and put them on the bright spots in the river. Five builds in a row: a bridge, a dock, a raft, steps and a base.

    BUILDING
  • 🤖

    Robo Route

    Build a chain of picture commands, press play and watch the robot carry a parcel to its station. Six levels, no reading.

  • ⚙️

    Robot Lab

    Build a program, run the robot, find bugs, open doors and collect batteries across eight missions.

    LOGIC
  • 🧺

    Garden Quest

    Walk the garden, collect fruit and vegetables and plan the route before the steps run out. Ten fixed maps, three stars on each.

    PUZZLE
  • 🧪

    Secret Rule Lab

    Study the sorting bot, pick a rule, predict the result and test your idea in six missions.

    SCIENCE
  • 🏘️

    Block Town

    Paint a whole town on a sheet of squared paper: roads join into crossings, water becomes a lake, and a little car sets off along a finished road.
